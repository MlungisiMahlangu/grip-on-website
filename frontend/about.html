About Grip On

Made by people who train.

Grip On started on a gym floor, not a design board. We make training wear for the moment your hands start to slip and your form is the only thing keeping the set alive.

The idea

No noise. Just gear that holds.

Every Grip On piece is built around one question: does this help the lift, or is it just decoration? That's why the range stays simple, heavyweight cotton for the Tshirts, real compression for the tights, and a logo you'll actually want to wear outside the gym too.

01

Tested under load

Every fabric is worn through real training blocks before it goes on the site, not just modeled.

02

Built to last seasons, not sets

Stitching and fabric weight chosen for years of washes, not just good first impressions.

03

Small batch, on purpose

We'd rather sell out of a colourway than compromise on the fabric to keep it always in stock.
